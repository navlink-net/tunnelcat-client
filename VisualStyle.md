# Visual Style Guidelines v1.0 — ShortNerdCat

**Status:** working version. **This revision fully replaces the previous guideline.**

NavLink's and The Tunnel Cat's colors were extracted from the raster references provided. Once the source logo files are available, the values should be cross-checked — until then they're treated as the working brand colors.

This ShortNerdCat section presents a proposed visual direction created specifically for this project.

---

## 1. Brand Architecture

NavLink, The Tunnel Cat, and ShortNerdCat are connected at the product level, but **visually are three independent brands**.

| Brand | Visual language |
|---|---|
| **NavLink** | Space-age neon technological futurism |
| **The Tunnel Cat** | Yellow-and-black industrial illustration and poster art |
| **ShortNerdCat** | Cozy modern computer-world with a black kitten |

Nothing is automatically carried over between brands:
- colors;
- typefaces;
- backgrounds;
- textures;
- button shapes;
- illustration style;
- decorative elements.

Their connection is shown through product names, copy, and logos placed side by side — never through visual blending.

## 4. ShortNerdCat

### 4.1. Status

This section is a newly proposed style.

ShortNerdCat does not copy Minecraft's look. Minecraft exists only as the character's hobby and a source of storylines.

### 4.2. Name

Correct spelling: **ShortNerdCat**
Acceptable short form: **SNC**

The short form is used: in the UI; in the icon; in technical documentation; after the first full mention of the name in a document.

Not acceptable: Short Nerd Cat; Shortnerdcat; Short NerdCat; SNCat.

### 4.3. Proposed Visual Style

Core direction: **a cozy modern computer workshop, seen through the eyes of a curious black kitten.**

This is a blend of: soft cinematic illustration; a modern computer interface; a home workshop; a gaming hobby; warm desk-lamp light; cool monitor light.

The style is NOT: pixel art; voxel graphics; a Minecraft parody; cyberpunk; a children's cartoon; an interface for professional hackers.

### 4.4. SNC Visual Reference

The base scene that defines the direction:

> A black kitten sits in a slightly oversized graphite hoodie at a desk in a cozy computer room. In front of it are two monitors: one shows a terminal and a server diagram, the other an original building-and-exploration game set in a procedural world with no recognizable Minecraft elements. On the desk: a keyboard, circuit boards, a notebook with a network diagram, and a few plain wooden cubes. Cool blue monitor light lights the kitten's face, while a warm amber lamp glows to the side. The illustration is smooth, dimensional, lightly stylized — no pixel grid, no cubic characters.

This scene should become SNC's first primary mood reference.

### 4.5. SNC Color Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| `snc-bg-primary` | `#11141A` | Primary background |
| `snc-surface` | `#1B2028` | Cards and panels |
| `snc-kitten-black` | `#07090D` | Primary fur color |
| `snc-fur-highlight` | `#252B34` | Highlights on black fur |
| `snc-screen-cyan` | `#59C8FF` | Screen light and active elements |
| `snc-warm-amber` | `#FFB547` | Lamps, eyes, warm accents |
| `snc-soft-lime` | `#A6D66D` | Rare game/status accents |
| `snc-nerd-violet` | `#8B7CFF` | Secondary digital accent |
| `snc-text-primary` | `#F4F1E8` | Primary text |
| `snc-text-muted` | `#99A2B0` | Secondary text |

**Color distribution**
- 60–70% — dark graphite background;
- 15–20% — cool blue lighting;
- 8–12% — warm amber lighting;
- no more than 5% — green;
- no more than 5% — violet.

Green must not dominate, and must never be paired with brown, cubic "dirt block" ground.

### 4.6. SNC Logo

**Primary mark** — proposed construction: a black kitten's head; a compact silhouette; two pointed ears; warm amber eyes; a thin cyan screen-light rim; no pixels, cubes, or pickaxes. The mark sits inside a rounded square.

**App icon**
- format: `1:1`;
- background: `#1B2028`;
- corner radius: roughly **20% of the width**;
- the kitten's face fills **68–74% of the area**;
- eyes: `#FFB547`;
- cool rim light: `#59C8FF`;
- no text inside the primary app icon.

**Wordmark**

`ShortNerdCat` is set in **Manrope ExtraBold 800**.

The word is never split into three differently-colored parts. Only `Nerd` may be highlighted in `#59C8FF` in promotional materials — never in the primary logo.

**Minimum sizes**
- full logo: **140 px**;
- app icon: **32 px**;
- printed mark: **10 mm**.

### 4.7. SNC Typography

| Use | Typeface |
|---|---|
| Logo and large headlines | Manrope ExtraBold 800 |
| Headings | Manrope Bold 700 |
| UI | Manrope Medium 500 |
| Body text | Manrope Regular 400 |
| Terminal and technical labels | JetBrains Mono Medium 500 |

**Forbidden:** pixel fonts; Minecraft-imitating fonts; faux-8-bit headlines; decorative square letterforms; a green outline around text.

### 4.8. The ShortNerdCat Character

**Fixed traits**

Black kitten; compact build; head is roughly **30% of total height**; large but not infantile eyes; amber iris; black fur with graphite highlights; a long, expressive tail.

**Clothing**

Core outfit: a graphite hoodie `#1B2028`; a small blue detail — zipper, drawstring, or patch; simple dark pants; soft sneakers; headphones around the neck or nearby on the desk.

Glasses are allowed but not a mandatory recurring element.

**Recurring props**

Illustrations may include: a laptop; a keyboard; circuit boards; a small screwdriver; a notebook; headphones; a home server; network cables; a mug; a set of plain wooden cubes.

**Character**

SNC is: curious; focused; a bit shy; enthusiastic; self-reliant; technically capable; not an overconfident "super-hacker."

### 4.9. Depicting the Minecraft Hobby

Minecraft is part of the character's backstory, not the brand's graphic system.

**Allowed**
- the kitten plays Minecraft when the game is named in copy;
- the computer screen can be shown out of focus or at an angle;
- the kitten discusses game servers;
- the kitten writes a mod or configures a server;
- plain wooden cubes sit on the desk;
- shared themes of exploration, building, and procedural worlds are used;
- an original, abstract sandbox game with no recognizable assets can be shown.

**Forbidden**
- the Creeper or a similar green four-legged silhouette;
- Steve, Alex, Enderman, or other recognizable characters;
- cubic trees with recognizable proportions;
- the Minecraft grass-block texture;
- dirt, stone, ore, or TNT textures;
- the hotbar;
- hearts and the hunger bar;
- the official inventory;
- the crafting table;
- a pixel pickaxe;
- the Minecraft logo;
- a Minecraft-style font;
- a green-and-brown palette as the brand's base;
- language implying partnership with Mojang or Microsoft.

### 4.10. SNC Illustrations

**Style**

Smooth digital illustration; soft, rounded forms; moderately realistic fur; expressive facial expressions; a cozy setting; a mix of cool and warm light; a highly detailed desk; no pixel grid.

**Lighting**

Core scheme: cool monitor light from the left or front; warm amber lamp light from the right; a dark neutral background; eyes stay amber.

**Composition**

For a promotional illustration: the kitten occupies **35–45% of the width**; the desk and computer occupy **30–45%**; the screen never exceeds **20% of the image**; the screen does not show a detailed game UI; the frame should include 3–7 small props that reveal character.

**Core formats**

| Use | Ratio |
|---|---:|
| App icon | `1:1` |
| Social media | `4:5` |
| Desktop hero | `16:9` |
| Phone screen | `9:16` |
| In-app illustration | `3:2` |

## 5. Joint Brand Usage

### 5.1. Core rule

Brand styles are never mixed within a single layout.

E.g. when describing ShortNerdCat as a Tunnel Cat client: the bulk of the page is styled as SNC; the Tunnel Cat logo is placed as a separate element; the yellow-black industrial texture is not carried into the SNC interface as a whole.

When describing The Tunnel Cat as a NavLink product: the material stays yellow-black; the NavLink logo sits in its own neutral zone; NavLink's space flows are not added inside the tunnel.

### 5.2. Joint lockup

Order:
```
[product logo]     A NavLink product     [NavLink logo]
```
or:
```
[product logo]
A NavLink product
```
The phrase "A NavLink product" is set in the neutral typeface of whichever brand owns the page.

The NavLink logo may never be recolored into Tunnel Cat yellow or SNC's palette.

### 5.3. Spacing between logos

- the gap between logos is at least the height of the smaller logo;
- logos are never connected by lines;
- no tunnels, cables, stars, or game blocks are placed between them;
- each logo keeps its own clear space.

## 6. Quick Layout Check — ShortNerdCat

- Is the main character a black kitten?
- Are computers or a technical hobby visible in frame?
- Is the style smooth and modern rather than pixelated?
- Does the Minecraft hobby come through the story rather than copying the game?
- Is there contrast between cool screen light and warm home light?
